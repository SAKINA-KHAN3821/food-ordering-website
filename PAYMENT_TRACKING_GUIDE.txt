🎯 HOW TO USE:

PLACING AN ORDER:
1. Add items to cart
2. Click "Checkout" button
3. Review order summary
4. Select payment method:
   - Credit/Debit Card (enter card details)
   - UPI (instant payment)
   - Cash on Delivery (pay later)
   - Wallet (digital payment)
5. Click "Pay Securely"
6. Order placed! 🎉

TRACKING YOUR ORDER:
1. After payment, tracking modal opens automatically
2. See 5 stages:
   ✅ Order Placed
   ✅ Order Confirmed (2 min)
   ✅ Preparing Food (10 min)
   ✅ Out for Delivery (20 min)
   ✅ Delivered (30-45 min)

3. Each stage updates automatically
4. Get notifications for each status change

VIEW PAST ORDERS:
1. Click the receipt icon (bottom right)
2. See your latest order
3. Track delivery status
4. View order details
